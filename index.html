Why

Everyone should be able to have a voice model of themselves: one that handles the calls you don't have time for, and helps you stay in touch with the people you care about.

Jarvis started as an agent that answers my messages as me, with memory for every person built from years of conversation and self-review loops that improve it daily. Voice is the next channel.
