Fifty Years Out

An interactive AI futures globe: click a country and drag from its most dystopian to its most utopian 2076, each future grounded in that country's data and its own culture's idea of a good life.

Why I made it As a thought experiment: what would a good, or a bad, future with AI actually look like in a specific place, imagined from inside that culture rather than from Silicon Valley? Building it forces each future to be concrete enough to draw.
