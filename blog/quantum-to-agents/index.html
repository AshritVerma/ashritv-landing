From quantum labs to agents

September 16, 2026

In undergrad I was automating the coupling work in our lab. Then came the GPT moment, and after that I was building control software to automate neutral-atom experiments toward continuous quantum computing.

Seeing what AI could do, I started applying the same principles to shorter-term personal projects: FF Sidekick, SquishyGPT, and side-by-side thought partners like Gadfly.

Through those projects I kept running into the same problems with memory and recursive self-improvement. I didn't realize at first that they were open questions. That's what pulled me toward fundamental research on memory and self-improvement.

A side note: I was pleasantly surprised by how fast I could go. I could deploy in a day, let the codebase evolve itself, and have it ready for large-scale use within a week.
