The case for simple experiments

October 15, 2026

Something I appreciate about Michael Fee's lab is the simplicity and sharpness of the experiments, like cooling part of a songbird's brain to see what happens to its song.

I see myself working the same way: an extremely well-thought-out experiment, less convoluted than what we usually see in the field, that lets you draw many conclusions from it, iteratively.
