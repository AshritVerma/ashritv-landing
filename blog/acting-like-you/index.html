Making an AI act like you

October 20, 2026

When you want an AI to act like you, you want it to take on your persona. The problem is that RL pulls these models toward acting like an assistant.

That wouldn't be a problem if you, as the persona, did the RL yourself. But that can't happen before a user has even come on board.

What I've seen is that self-improvement starts out rough and then gains a lot of performance very quickly, even without anyone labeling what's good or bad. In a product there's no room for that early error. It has to work out of the box.

So: fine-tuning is what makes it work out of the box. Self-RL is how it keeps growing afterward without asking the user for input.
