AI as a long-term participant in our relationships

October 29, 2026

The question I keep coming back to is what happens when AI becomes a long-term participant in our relationships: it remembers, it speaks up, it learns us.

That question has a few parts:

  • How AI can make us better in our relationships, with other people and with ourselves.
  • What the longitudinal effects are, and how to run studies that actually measure them.
  • Agents that improve themselves over time alongside the people they work with, as those people change.

I've been running personal projects, and projects with other people, that sit right on this question. I think it's time to turn what I've learned from them into generalizable human-AI design knowledge.

I care more about the research than the revenue.
