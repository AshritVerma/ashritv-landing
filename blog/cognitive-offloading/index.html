Cognitive offloading

October 22, 2026

From Martha Minow's lecture, "Constituting Democracy in the Era of AI."

Cognitive offloading was already here with algorithmic culture. The bigger risk now is never developing critical skills at all, because we offload the thinking and with it the need for the skills. People retain more when they're required to do the thinking.

That's an opportunity to build better, trustworthy tools for younger people, and universities should take the lead.

On news: we need to share the same assumptions. Algorithms push misinformation and polarization, and news is part of what gives us a sense of "we."

On fairness: the benefits of technological progress don't trickle down equally.
